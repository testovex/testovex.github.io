Privacy Policy

How Testovex collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: July 3, 2026
Testovex is currently in beta. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Testovex platform, including beta participants, and will be updated as the product evolves.

1. Who We Are

Testovex ("we", "us", "our") is a no-code test automation platform. Our service is accessible at testovex.com. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@testovex.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Account Information

  • Name and email address when you register or join the waitlist
  • Organisation name and role
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — never in plain text)

Usage Data

  • Test cases, test suites, and test objects you create within the platform
  • Test run results, screenshots, videos, and trace files generated during runs
  • Browser and OS type used to access the platform
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited and features used within the application

Browser Extension Data

  • The Testovex Recorder extension captures user interactions (clicks, typing, navigation) on websites you choose to test. This data is sent only to your Testovex account and is not used for any other purpose.
  • The extension does not capture data from websites when recording is not actively enabled.

Communications

  • Emails you send to hello@testovex.com
  • Feedback or support requests submitted within the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Testovex platform
  • Create and manage your account and organisation
  • Send you important service notices, updates, and security alerts
  • Respond to your support requests and questions
  • Send product updates and beta programme communications (you can opt out at any time)
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve features and fix bugs
  • Enforce our Terms of Use and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt)
  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • Daily automated backups of all platform data

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Testovex account.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within your organisation: Data you create is visible to other members of your Testovex organisation as configured by your organisation admin.
  • Service providers: We may use trusted third-party services to help operate the platform (e.g. email delivery, hosting). These providers are contractually required to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

6. Cookies

Testovex uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in to your account (session cookies)
  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how users interact with the platform

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of the platform.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

Test run artifacts (videos, screenshots, traces) are retained for 90 days and then automatically deleted unless you choose to archive them.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Portability: Request an export of your test data in a standard format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@testovex.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Testovex is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or by displaying a notice within the platform. Your continued use of Testovex after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@testovex.com
  • Website: testovex.com
