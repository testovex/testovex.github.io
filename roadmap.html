FAQ
Questions QA leaders ask
Does the app I'm testing need to be reachable over the internet?

For running tests, yes. The Chrome recorder runs in your browser, so it captures tests on any app you can open — including internal, staging, VPN-only, or localhost sites. But test runs execute on Testovex's cloud servers, which reach your app over the public internet. So a VPN-only or private app records fine, but the run can't reach it — point runs at an internet-reachable URL (e.g. a public staging environment). For internal-only apps, Enterprise self-hosted execution runs tests inside your own network.

Is my test data and credentials secure?

Yes. Every organization is fully isolated with role-based access control, and sensitive values live in locked org-level variables that are never exposed in test steps or logs. Each run's artifacts (video, screenshots, traces) are scoped to your org. Enterprise adds SSO/SAML and a self-hosted option.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. QA analysts build tests by clicking through your app with the Chrome recorder. Control flow (loops, if/else), reusable components, hover and iframe handling, and variables are all no-code. Playwright runs it all under the hood — you get the power without writing scripts.

How long until my first test is running?

Minutes. Install the Chrome recorder, click through a flow, and run it. Most teams have a real regression suite live on their first day — and your free founder-led onboarding session gets you there faster.

What happens to pricing after the beta?

Testovex is completely free during beta — no credit card required to join. Beta founders lock in 40% off any paid plan for their first year. You'll get clear advance notice before any plan begins billing; there are no surprise charges.

What if a UI change breaks my tests?

The smart locator engine uses 9 strategies to find elements, so most tests survive UI changes automatically. When something does need attention, fix a reusable component once and every test inherits it — and version history plus milestones let you roll back test state instantly.

Does it fit our existing CI/CD?

Yes. Trigger runs from any pipeline or terminal via the CLI and API, with parallel Playwright execution. Use runs as quality gates so defects are caught before they reach production.

For enterprise & security teams
Do you support SSO / SAML and centralized user management?

Yes — on the Enterprise plan, Testovex supports SSO/SAML so access is governed by your identity provider, with role-based access control across every organization and project. Provisioning and de-provisioning are centralized, so off-boarding a team member removes their access everywhere at once.

Can Testovex run in our own environment or region?

Yes. Enterprise includes a self-hosted deployment option for teams with data-residency, network-isolation, or VPC requirements. For everyone else Testovex is a fully managed cloud service — and either way, your data stays isolated to your organization.

How do you handle data security and security reviews?

Every organization is fully isolated, sensitive values live in locked variables that never appear in logs or test steps, and all traffic is encrypted in transit over TLS. As part of an Enterprise evaluation we'll complete your vendor security questionnaire and walk your team through our security posture.

What compliance standards and certifications do you hold?

Testovex is built to enterprise security best practices — tenant isolation, role-based access control, audit logging, and encryption in transit. As we graduate from beta, formal certifications such as SOC 2 are on our roadmap; we're glad to share our current security documentation and complete your compliance questionnaire on request.

Do you provide audit logs and governance controls?

Yes. Business and Enterprise plans include audit logs, advanced RBAC, and org-level governance — a shared reusable-component library and locked organizational standards keep multiple teams consistent, and version history plus milestones capture every change for full traceability.

What SLA and support do enterprise customers get?

Enterprise includes a service-level agreement, priority support, and a dedicated success manager who owns your onboarding and ongoing adoption. Business plans receive priority support, and every plan includes run diagnostics (video, screenshots, and traces) so issues are fast to resolve.

Can we sign an MSA / DPA and set custom procurement terms?

Yes. Enterprise supports custom contracts (MSA), a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), a security review, custom integrations, and annual invoicing. Email hello@testovex.com and we'll start the process.
